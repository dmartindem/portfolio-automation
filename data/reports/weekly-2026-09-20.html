Recommendations

1. Redirect this month's contributions toward cash and away from the growth funds. Confidence: moderate.
The Fed has started hiking and the market expects more. SPAXX now pays a real return for the first time in this account's life, and every dollar that goes there instead of VUG/VOOG/QQQ reduces NVDA and mega-cap look-through weight at zero tax cost. That is the cheapest lever you have and it is the one to exhaust before considering any trim.

2. Close the four decayed Jan-2027 calls (AUR, ACHR, QS, SIRI). Confidence: high.
117 days out, ~0.04% of the account, near-certain total losses. Closing books the loss on your timetable rather than at expiry and clears the ladder. This realises a loss, not a gain — no tax friction.
Counterargument: the loss is identical whether taken now or at expiry, and commissions on four near-zero positions may exceed the benefit. This is tidiness with a small option value, not alpha.
