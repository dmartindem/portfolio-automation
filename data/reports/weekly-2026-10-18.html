Recommendations

1. Make the Jan-2027 ASTS call decision this week. Confidence: high.
The 90-DTE escalation date passed on Friday after being flagged in six consecutive reviews, and the fundamentals of the decision changed in the same week: the $35 strike's cushion compressed from roughly two-thirds to roughly a quarter. Time value decays fastest from here. A written rule — roll, exercise, or close at a named ASTS level — removes the decision from a bad moment later.
Counterargument: BlueBirds 14–16 are at the Cape, so the single most likely upside catalyst sits inside the contract life. Acting on a 52-week-low week is how people sell the bottom of their highest-conviction position.
Tax: these are up +236% to +369%, so closing them realises almost the entire proceeds as gain, very likely short-term — the most expensive rung in the ladder on a friction basis. That argues for rolling over closing if the thesis is intact. Selling realises it; holding does not. Fold in closing the four decayed AUR/ACHR/QS/SIRI calls at the same expiry — near-certain total losses, near-zero cost to close, and the harvest is free.

2. Point October and November contributions at cash or non-growth. Confidence: moderate.

Counterargument: this is market timing dressed as risk management. Buying growth funds after a −3.64% Nasdaq week is buying lower, and on a long horizon the drag from sitting in cash usually exceeds the benefit of the diversification.
