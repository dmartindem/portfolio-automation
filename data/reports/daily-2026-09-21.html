Recommendations

1. Point new money away from large-cap growth. Confidence: high. Your monthly contribution is the only lever that reshapes concentration at zero tax cost. Directing it away from the growth funds and the mega-cap block reduces look-through concentration without realising a cent of gain. Counterargument: it is slow — contributions move a book this size by fractions of a point per month, and it leaves the existing concentration entirely untouched. A rising-rate tape is also exactly when growth gets cheaper, so deliberately under-buying it may cost return. Tax: contributions realise nothing.

With the 18 Nov print still two months out, there is no forcing event. Counterargument: tax friction is not a reason to hold something you would not buy today, and 15% in one name into a high-variance earnings date is genuine single-name risk.
