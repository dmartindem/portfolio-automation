Recommendations

Everything above this line is measured or sourced. Everything below it is interpretation. It is not financial advice, it has no view of your tax situation or anything outside this portfolio, and it can be wrong.

1. Direct this month's contribution away from large-cap growth. Confidence: high. The mega-cap 8 is 50.37% look-through and the index sleeve concentrates rather than diversifies it. New money reshapes that at zero tax cost, which no trade can match. Counterargument: that concentration is where the returns came from, and deliberately buying lower-expected-return assets is a real cost paid now against a risk that may never arrive.

2. Do not trim NVDA ahead of tomorrow's print. Confidence: moderate. Gain share of proceeds is 79.2%, the highest in the book — a sale surrenders roughly four-fifths of proceeds as taxable gain, and that is a poor way to express a view on a single binary event. If the exposure genuinely feels too large, the lever is the contribution above, not the sale. Counterargument: a 15% all-in position going into the highest-variance event in the book, four days into an unplanned CEO transition with a public campaign to reinstate the former CEO, is exactly the setup where paying for a hedge rather than selling is easiest to justify. Note also that the acknowledged weights date from 10 Sep and NVDA is down about a third from its October peak, so 15.05% is probably stale on the high side — the weekly run should refresh before any sizing decision leans on it.

3. Decide the ASTS Jan-2027 ladder on your own schedule, not in January. Confidence: high on timing, no view on direction. Deep-ITM calls at 60 DTE hold little extrinsic value, so roll-versus-exercise is close to a pure financing choice and gets worse to execute as liquidity thins into expiry. Separately, the four decayed AUR/ACHR/QS/SIRI calls are near-certain total losses; closing them before 31 Dec harvests the loss at almost no cost. Tax: closing the ASTS calls realises gain (the baseline does not compute a gain share for them, so there is no ratio to quote); exercising instead starts a fresh holding period at the strike. The three Jan-2028 calls hold 58% of the harvestable inventory but carry an unsettled §1091 wash-sale question against your long stock — that one needs your preparer, not this report. Counterargument: the 15 Jan expiry sits directly on top of the year-end service milestone and the Q1 2027 launches, so acting early forgoes exactly the convexity the position exists to capture.
